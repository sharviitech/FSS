Who we are

Founded by families. Led by people who know this loss.

Families for Safe Streets was founded in New York City in 2014 by people who lost loved ones or were seriously injured in traffic crashes. Today our members support each other, share their stories and organize for life-saving change across the country.

What we're fighting for

Our members' stories move decision-makers to act. These are our national priorities.

Safe Vehicles

Stop Super Speeders and autonomous vehicle safety.

Safe Vehicles →

Safe Roads

Designing roads to encourage safe driving and mitigate human error is one of the most important ways to address this crisis.

Safe Roads →

Safe Speeds

Unsafe speeds are a well-documented factor in death and injury, especially among people outside of a vehicle.

Safe Speeds →

Featured campaign

Stop Super Speeders

Laws requiring Intelligent Speed Assistance for drivers with repeated extreme speeding violations. Nine jurisdictions now have these laws, six of them passed in 2026.
